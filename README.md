# Snafu & Fubar + Private Intelligence

**Independent Intelligence · Investigation · Analysis**

> Investigate. Analyse. Illuminate.

Snafu & Fubar is an independent private intelligence practice. We provide deep-dive research and analysis on geopolitics and technology for decisions that require more than the immediate account. 

We are a specialist boutique. Our centre of gravity is the intersection of policy, infrastructure, security, and capability claims. We are hired, most often, to be the independent second opinion: the party with no stake in the answer, whose only obligation is to the evidence.

---

### 🎯 Core Method

We do not manufacture certainty or guarantee a pre-determined conclusion. We examine questions systematically, assess available evidence, and communicate findings with absolute integrity—keeping observation, inference, and hypothesis strictly separate.

**Define → Collect → Verify → Analyse → Report**

- **Investigative rather than assumption-led.** The question leads; prior belief does not.
- **Open to competing explanations and correction.**
- **Focused on useful findings, not dramatic presentation.**
- **Proportionate to the evidence.** Scope and conclusions rise and fall together.

---

### 🛠 Capabilities

- **Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT):** Public-record research, source assessment, digital-footprint and timeline research.
- **Investigative Research & Due Diligence:** Corporate background, relationship analysis, reputational due diligence.
- **Market & Competitive Intelligence:** Sector research, competitor analysis, supply-chain shifts.
- **Geopolitical, Security & Technology Research:** AI and computing infrastructure, defence, cyber-security, capability claims.
- **Independent Intelligence Assessment:** Cross-source comparison, emerging risk assessments.

---

### 🛡️ Standards

A compelling story is not proof. A pattern does not, by itself, establish causation. Confidence should follow the quality of the evidence, not the force of the narrative.

- **Evidence before assertion.**
- **Verification before confidence.**
- **Context before interpretation.**
- **Inference kept visible.**
- **Uncertainty acknowledged.**
- **Responsible investigation.**

*Lawful methods only. We work from open and lawfully obtained sources. We do not conduct surveillance, hacking, pretexting, or covert human intelligence.*
